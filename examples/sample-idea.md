# Idea v1

<!-- Sample idea for the demo and for teams who want a test run first. -->

**KnygųMainas** — an app where high-school students buy and sell used textbooks and exam-prep notes from older students at their own school.
Students who finished a year list their books and notes with a photo and a price, and younger students reserve them and meet at school to pay.
Students save money, older students earn some, and fewer books end up in the trash.
We make money by taking 10% of each sale.
We start with one gymnasium in Vilnius and expand to other schools.
